# Expandive

An iPad whiteboard for handwriting, folding away detail and recording visual explanations.

[Expandive at Burrito’s Labs](https://burritoslabs.com/catalogue/expandive/)

## Technology

iPadOS, Apple Pencil, on-device media recording and optional iCloud.

## Architecture

Boards organize editable ink, images and named foldable excerpts. Recorded strokes and audio can be replayed or exported independently of the editable board.

## Product behavior

People can work offline. Optional iCloud uses their Apple account, and video or audio export is created on the iPad.
